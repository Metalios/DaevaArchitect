        In-game setup

          Keep these manual

          During safe damage, keep the filler and in-game Macro key held while you press or hold manual skills. Manual input takes priority. Release Macro to stop its casts; release the filler too when all attacks must stop for mechanics or urgent healing. Keep charged attacks, toggles, movement and emergency skills on manual keys. A 10 ms macro step is an input delay, not a guaranteed cancel time; Global has minimum cancel delays. Test on a dummy and adjust for your ping. In AION 1 control mode, use a keyboard Macro bind.

          Equip one title in each of the Attack, Defense and Other slots. Every title you earn also adds its collection effect permanently, even after you equip another. Check only titles you actually own; this is a focused progression list, not the full catalog.

          Fast collection wins

          1. Finish the Hero Episode story and join a Legion for easy owned effects.
          2. Use the Spacetime Rift 3 times for Hello There; 50 uses earns Nice to Meet You.
          3. Find Empyrean Traces to raise your faction's Monolith to 20 for Guardian and 30 for Saint.
          4. Clear the Sealed Dungeon Ruins of the Ancient City of Roah for Through Hell and Back.

          Longer goals: all Genus Insight at level 10 for Unbound by Genus; 200 pets for Rule All Creation; 25 wings for Dominator of Dimensions; 700 skins for Empty Closet.
