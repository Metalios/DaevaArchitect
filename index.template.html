    01 / THE RULE

    Trigger Judgment. Spend it immediately.

    Shield Smite, Warding Strike and Doom Shield open a brief Judgment window. Letting a second trigger fire before you use Judgment can waste the first window. Keep boss control and survival on manual keys.

        Set it up in game

        1. Open Settings (O) → Key Settings → General → Macro and bind a comfortable keyboard or side-mouse key.
        2. In Skills (K), stack the skills on two hotbar slots as shown. Row 0 is the slot's first priority; keep the no-cooldown Pummel below the conditional attacks.
        3. Open Skill Macros → Add Macro. Add the E hotbar slot as step 1 and RMB hotbar slot as step 2. Start both delays at 10 ms.
        4. Hold the macro key during safe uptime. If Vicious Strike is on left mouse, hold it alongside the macro to weave basic attacks. Release for mechanics and press your manual keys.

        Keep these on manual keys

        Taunt, Shield of Protection, Doom Shield, Punishment, Defiance and movement need your timing. Doom Shield can move you and turn the boss, so leave it out of the default tank macro.

        The client says a held macro repeatedly attempts its steps and does not guarantee strict order. Manual input takes precedence. Test the stack on a dummy and adjust delay for your ping; 10 ms is a starting value, not a measured optimum. A mouse macro bind will not work in AION 1 control mode.

        Point economics
