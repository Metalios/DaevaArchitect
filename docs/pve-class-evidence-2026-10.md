## Scoring policy

The class evaluator in `js/engine/class_models.js` supplies repeatable *relative outcome indices*: sustained, burst, survival, uptime and party support. It only awards configured breakpoint interactions if the rank is reached **and the corresponding Specialty option is selected**. Source-backed skill/Stigma relationships determine which effects qualify, while the numeric strength of the effects is deliberately marked uncalibrated.

It does not use ranking/Combat Power as a substitute for measured DPS, never synthesizes missing gear, and does not claim an absolute optimum. The search in `js/optimizer/objectives.js` retains multiple connected path seeds and compares fully budgeted candidates; it excludes PvP-only stats in PvE.

### Critical next evidence to collect

Record paired fight logs from the same region and patch including class, exact gear, purchased nodes, Mastery ranks, Stigma ranks, chosen Specialties, boss, duration, positional uptime, damage/healing breakdown, resource state and team composition. Multiple repeats are required per candidate; compare normalized output and variance. Otherwise report *modeled* rankings only.

Mark uncertain performance parameters with a version and invalidate them whenever Global skills or patches change. Do not infer that repeatedly copied popular builds are independent performance observations.
